# 06 — User Feedback & Evaluation Instrument

**Status:** Active deployment phase — survey instrument and consent templates prepared for University of Camarines Norte College of Engineering field testing.

## Data Privacy Compliance (RA 10173)
Participation is voluntary, informed consent is obtained prior to testing, and all responses are anonymized and used strictly for academic evaluation in compliance with the **Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**.

## Survey Structure
1. **System Usability Scale (SUS):** Standard 10-item Likert scale (1 = Strongly Disagree to 5 = Strongly Agree) measuring ease of use and interface clarity.
2. **Perspective Drawing Comprehension Items:**
   - *The AR guide helped me visualize where the horizon line sits relative to my drawing surface.*
   - *Adjusting 1-Point and 2-Point vanishing points helped me understand how parallel edges converge.*
   - *The Trace Photo mode and Clear White background removal helped me compare proportions on paper.*
